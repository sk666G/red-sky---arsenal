# Red Sky — Legal

Authorized use only.

## What this is

Red Sky is a modular post-exploitation framework built for red-team engagements, CTF competitions, lab research, and security education on systems the operator owns or has written permission to test.

## What this is not

Red Sky is not authorized for use against systems you do not own or have explicit written permission to test. Running the C2, deploying a beacon, scanning a network, harvesting credentials, phishing a target, or interacting with any system without permission is illegal in most jurisdictions.

## The operator responsibility

The operator is the only person responsible for how Red Sky is used. The authors of this tool do not control who clones this repository, what targets they point it at, or what they do with the output.

Before running any module against any system, confirm:

1. You own the system, OR
2. You have a signed engagement letter, scope document, or written authorization from someone who owns the system, AND
3. The authorization covers the specific action you are about to take (scanning, exploitation, credential access, persistence, exfiltration, etc.)

If any of the above is not true, do not run it.

## Legal frameworks you might be under

Depends on your country. Common ones:

- United States: Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA), 18 U.S.C. 1030
- United Kingdom: Computer Misuse Act 1990
- European Union: Directive 2013/40/EU on attacks against information systems
- France: Code pénal, articles 323-1 through 323-7
- Germany: Strafgesetzbuch 202a through 202c
- Canada: Criminal Code, sections 342.1 and 430

This list is not exhaustive and is not legal advice. If you are unsure, consult a lawyer in your jurisdiction before running any offensive tool.

## Bugs, disclosure, and misuse reports

If you find a vulnerability in Red Sky itself, or you believe this repository is being used to attack systems without authorization, open an issue or contact the maintainer.
